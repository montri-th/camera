# CityMETER Camera Shops — owner preferences

Created 2026-10-22 (release 0.5.0) from the CityMETER Luxury Brands 0.5 preferences, which this product shares (“ทำแบบเดียวกัน”). Camera-specific rules are marked **Camera**.

- Apply current LDS 0.9.7 (v0.9.7-owner.1, standalone-0.9.7-r1) and preserve the map-first CityMETER layout. Colours come only from `assets/theme.css` and `js/scales.js`; never approximate a value by eye. Owner rule 2026-10-22: a colour scale always uses the full 41-sample LDS scale — `buildCountScale` maps a count linearly from 1 to the selection maximum onto all 41 `count` samples (no 5-class projection); legends show the 41-sample ramp; numbers on samples 27–29 (below 4.5:1 with both inks) sit on a surface chip.
- Aha first, in 3D: the first paint is the city preset (pitch 56°, bearing 28°) framing the top three neighborhoods, focused automatically (`auto` focus mode, no `areas` param). Show one computed spatial finding (share of shop banners with an outlet in those areas), its numerator and denominator, the 3D map cue and the immediate actions (ดูทุกย่าน, เปรียบเทียบย่าน) before complex filters. No onboarding gate. Snapshot numbers in the handoff JSON are fixtures, not UI constants. **Camera:** on wide screens the overview is never framed below zoom 13 (the city massing starts there) and the pitched fit keeps the legend clear; where no Locale holds a row (provinces outside Bangkok) the finding moves to the leading venue that has a host point (share of banners at that venue); with no such venue it falls back to the plain count.
- The product statement is a winning strategy (Who / What / Which / How per MBB v0.5.3 §7.2 and PB v6 §1.6) — read `PRODUCT_STATEMENT.md` before changing what the first screen argues.
- **Camera — units:** ร้าน = a distinct shop banner (BIG CAMERA, Sony Store …), สาขา = one outlet record, สถานที่ = venue, ย่าน = CityMETER production Locale. Never “แบรนด์” as a unit: in this domain it reads as a camera maker. A co-branded storefront is ONE outlet whose `brands` lists every counted banner (retail and host-counter banners; an operator is not a banner); banner counts take each banner once. A segment filter keeps an outlet when any of its banners is in the segment and counts only those banners (`projectSegment` returns a copy, never a mutation).
- **Camera — ย่าน:** `data/neighborhoods.geojson` holds the production Locales (ids = Locale uuids) that contain a camera venue; point-in-polygon on them must equal production `base/locatePoint` for every Bangkok host point (the data build stops otherwise). Never invent a Locale outside Bangkok; never assign by name or nearest boundary.
- **Camera — hosts:** the research master decides every host point and host building (venue join in `02_STORES` / `07_VENUES` / `12_SPATIAL_JOIN`, building join in `13_BUILDINGS` and its feature file; revision 2026-10-22 13:13 UTC). The pipeline (`scripts/host-links/03_verify_master_join.py`, `scripts/build-camera-data.py`) verifies the join against CityMETER production and maps it — it never adds, moves or upgrades a join. Coordinates are CityMETER host-record points (`centers[].kind`; this release: Shopping Center only, the Grocery/hypermarket kind stays supported) and are not shopfronts. Venues the master holds (`venueJoin: held`) keep no point and show “พิกัดรอตรวจ”; app crosswalk candidates go to `host_link_proposals.csv` for the research team, never to public data. Missing stays missing: no geocoding of addresses, no coordinates read from unaudited map links, no nearest-neighbour joins; a row without a point shows “—” for ย่าน, never “นอกย่าน”.
- Focus + quiet context: draw detail only for the focused part — the selected neighborhood, the explicit or automatic focus areas, or everything only when the user asks for ดูทุกย่าน (`?areas=all`). Everything else stays visible but quiet: outline-only areas, quiet labels, grey context dots without labels and outside the tab order, city massing in the quiet tone.
- Basemap and massing are context, never data: colours come only from the `--basemap-*` tokens; the style is generated by `js/map-style.js`; building heights from OpenFreeMap/OSM tiles are never quoted as dataset facts. Host buildings (`js/buildings.js`) remain the only building layer with a data colour.
- Camera presets (`js/camera.js`) never move or scale data: `city` for the overview, `model` (FOV 14°) for a neighborhood, `eye` (มุมสถาปนิก, pitch 75 / FOV 24) at venue level only, `plan` when 3D is off. Keep every 3D preset at pitch ≤ 90° − fov/2 − 3° (MapLibre 5.6.2 fill-extrusion defect beyond).
- Venue level is a focus of its own: only the venue's rows carry detail — **Camera:** taken from the filtered scope, so a venue outside the automatic top three still gets its detail. Its host building is the only blue one and stays visible and clickable at any pan, pitch or zoom (host extrusion installed before every massing tier; `js/venue-xray.js` draws the solid on top and hit-tests its own triangles). Never reorder these layers casually. **Camera:** a host without an accepted building is framed on its host point and the legend hides the “อาคารของสถานที่” key.
- Country view: with the whole country on screen the provinces that hold camera shops (64, `data/provinces.geojson`, OpenGISData-Thailand CC BY 4.0) get frames filled by their own count classes below zoom 9, outlines to zoom 12, ink-chip labels and a panel list (ten rows, the rest folded) — each a drill-down into the จังหวัด filter. Frames are navigation context, never an administrative claim.
- Pictograms (owner test exception 2026-10-22): metric tiles, section headings and province chips use the 8-glyph Material Symbols Rounded pictogram subset (`PICTOGRAM_CONTRACT`; **Camera:** ร้าน = `photo_camera` U+E412, rebuilt byte-reproducibly by `scripts/build-pictogram-font.py`); the text label stays the meaning; the nav-7 subset stays byte-identical.
- Venue logos: `data/venue-logos.json` + `assets/venue-logos/` from the research sheet tab 10_VENUE_LOGOS (sha256 per the sheet); lookup by CityMETER host id, then exact venue label — never fuzzy; the venue name stays the identity; collecting the files is not a trademark licence.
- **Camera — store labels:** no store-banner logo is collected (`data/brand-logos.json` meta.status `not_collected`). Pins at zoom ≥ 14 carry the banner NAME as a light text chip (`BANNER_LABELS`: leading words or a research alias for names over 18 characters) — a name, never a substitute logo; collision-managed with the 250 ceiling, focused rows only.
- Breadcrumb is ประเทศไทย › จังหวัด › ย่าน › สถานที่ with the production province codes in `js/model.js#PROVINCES` (all 77, from production `base/info/lv0`); the ย่าน crumb comes from point-in-polygon on the venue's representative point; a store is the panel heading, never a crumb.
- Three row scopes are a contract: `rows` drive pins, counters and the table; `scopeRows` (filters before any area selection) drive the Aha denominator, the choropleth, the charts and the exclusive/shared reference.
- One marker always represents one outlet; never aggregate stores.
- Public files carry no phone numbers (the data build scrubs them from research notes) and no Drive/private URLs.
- Keep generic caveats and research workflow residue out of ordinary UI; preserve provenance and methodology in source documentation. Preserve factual status (including the hold labels: ตัวตนหน้าร้านรอตรวจ, รวมรายการรอตรวจ, ที่ตั้งขัดกัน, ที่อยู่ติดต่อ), dates, sources and coordinate precision; never show missing as zero.
- Use approved LDS Rounded outline navigation glyphs (FILL 0, weight 300, GRAD 0). The menu icon is for the mobile data sheet only.
- Copy: short, natural Thai, no caveats on screen; units, provenance and limits live in the data section, help and docs. Neighborhood labels are ink chips (`--area-label-*`); pin name chips are light cards so they never look like area labels.
- Charts: matrix cells are equal in size; only the number and its count class carry meaning. No CSV download control. Review actual Thai at desktop and narrow widths in light and dark themes before claiming a change is done; local renders are not proof of a live release — run `npm run verify:live`.
