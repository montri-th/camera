# CityMETER Camera Shops — owner preferences

Created 2026-10-22; data/asset contracts refreshed 2026-10-23 (0.5.1) from the CityMETER Luxury Brands 0.5 preferences, which this product shares (“ทำแบบเดียวกัน”). Camera-specific rules are marked **Camera**.

- Apply current LDS 0.9.7 (v0.9.7-owner.1, standalone-0.9.7-r1) and preserve the map-first CityMETER layout. Colours come only from `assets/theme.css` and `js/scales.js`; never approximate a value by eye. Owner rule 2026-10-22: a colour scale always uses the full 41-sample LDS scale — `buildCountScale` maps a count linearly from 1 to the selection maximum onto all 41 `count` samples (no 5-class projection); legends show the 41-sample ramp; numbers on samples 27–29 (below 4.5:1 with both inks) sit on a surface chip.
- Aha first, in 3D: the first paint is the city preset (pitch 56°, bearing 28°) framing the top three neighborhoods, focused automatically (`auto` focus mode, no `areas` param). Show one computed spatial finding (share of shop banners with an outlet in those areas), its numerator and denominator, the 3D map cue and the immediate actions (ดูทุกย่าน, เปรียบเทียบย่าน) before complex filters. No onboarding gate. Snapshot numbers in the handoff JSON are fixtures, not UI constants. **Camera:** on wide screens the overview is never framed below zoom 13 (the city massing starts there) and the pitched fit keeps the legend clear; where no Locale holds a row (provinces outside Bangkok) the finding moves to the leading venue that has a host point (share of banners at that venue); with no such venue it falls back to the plain count.
- The product statement is a winning strategy (Who / What / Which / How per MBB v0.5.3 §7.2 and PB v6 §1.6) — read `PRODUCT_STATEMENT.md` before changing what the first screen argues.
- **Camera — units (owner override 2026-10-23):** แบรนด์ = a distinct shop banner (BIG CAMERA, Sony Store …), สาขา = one outlet record, ศูนย์การค้า = the public label for a persistent named venue, ย่าน = CityMETER production Locale. This explicitly supersedes the earlier ร้าน/สถานที่ wording; source fields, named entities and business categories keep their meanings. A co-branded storefront is ONE outlet whose `brands` lists every counted banner (retail and host-counter banners; an operator is not a banner); banner counts take each banner once. A segment filter keeps an outlet when any of its banners is in the segment and counts only those banners (`projectSegment` returns a copy, never a mutation).
- **Camera — ย่าน:** `data/neighborhoods.geojson` holds production Locale UUIDs. Point-in-polygon uses each selected source-backed outlet/host point and must agree with production `base/locatePoint` receipts; missing remains missing. Do not require every outlet point to equal a host point. Never invent a Locale outside Bangkok or assign by name/nearest boundary. Locale Insight supplies context only, not official population, eligibility, risk or observed behavior.
- **Camera — hosts and selected points (0.5.1):** the refreshed research master owns IDs, source roles, selected points and accepted venue/building links. `scripts/refresh-camera-master.py` is the current adapter; `scripts/host-links/03_verify_master_join.py` and `scripts/build-camera-data.py` retain the historical 0.5.0 host-only contract. A sourced business point may exist without a venue or building. Never copy it into a venue point unless the source explicitly identifies a host-reference role. Keep the actual `geometryRole`, source URL/period and precision; an owner-linked map must identify the business/venue destination, never its viewport. A held host may coexist with a valid independent business point. No geocoded/nearest-host upgrade or synthetic geometry. Recompute every claimed containment after changing a point; preserve a verified named host as context when the point is outside and exclude it from inside totals. Unknown height stays null and display fallback stays separate. No point/geometry result establishes an entrance, floor, current operation or stock.
- Focus + quiet context: draw detail only for the focused part — the selected neighborhood, the explicit or automatic focus areas, or everything only when the user asks for ดูทุกย่าน (`?areas=all`). Everything else stays visible but quiet: outline-only areas, quiet labels, grey context dots without labels and outside the tab order, city massing in the quiet tone.
- Basemap and massing are context, never data: colours come only from the `--basemap-*` tokens; the style is generated by `js/map-style.js`; building heights from OpenFreeMap/OSM tiles are never quoted as dataset facts. Host buildings (`js/buildings.js`) remain the only building layer with a data colour.
- Camera presets (`js/camera.js`) never move or scale data: `city` for the overview, `model` (FOV 14°) for a neighborhood, `eye` (มุมสถาปนิก, pitch 75 / FOV 24) at venue level only, `plan` when 3D is off. Keep every 3D preset at pitch ≤ 90° − fov/2 − 3° (MapLibre 5.6.2 fill-extrusion defect beyond).
- Venue level is a focus of its own: only the venue's rows carry detail — **Camera:** taken from the filtered scope, so a venue outside the automatic top three still gets its detail. Its host building is the only blue one and stays visible and clickable at any pan, pitch or zoom (host extrusion installed before every massing tier; `js/venue-xray.js` draws the solid on top and hit-tests its own triangles). Never reorder these layers casually. **Camera:** a host without an accepted building is framed on its host point and the legend hides the “อาคารของศูนย์การค้า” key.
- Country view: with the whole country on screen all 77 provinces (`data/provinces.geojson`, pinned OpenGISData-Thailand CC BY 4.0) get frames filled by their own count classes below zoom 9, outlines to zoom 12, ink-chip labels and a panel list (ten rows, the rest folded) — each a drill-down into the จังหวัด filter, including provinces without observed rows. Zero means “ยังไม่พบสาขาในชุดข้อมูล”, never verified market absence. `count-metric` (`?metric=brands|pos`) switches source-backed unique banners / unique outlet IDs together across maps, legends, badges and rankings; all original 64 polygons remain byte-identical. Frames are navigation context, never an administrative claim.
- Pictograms (owner test exception 2026-10-22): metric tiles, section headings and province chips use the 8-glyph Material Symbols Rounded pictogram subset (`PICTOGRAM_CONTRACT`; **Camera:** แบรนด์ = `photo_camera` U+E412, rebuilt byte-reproducibly by `scripts/build-pictogram-font.py`); the text label stays the meaning; the nav-7 subset stays byte-identical.
- **Camera — logo files:** use `data/brand-logos.json`, `data/venue-logos.json` and their verified local originals/manifests. Keep retail banner, manufacturer, operator, venue-specific, corporate and parent-project roles separate. Join stable entity IDs/accepted host IDs or exact recorded labels; never fuzzy-propagate a venue mark. Source asset URL is not a stored file. Preserve original bytes, SHA-256, proportions, transparency and reviewed backing; no redraw/recolour. Missing/unsuitable/wrong-identity marks stay explicitly missing. Collection does not grant trademark rights; public assets contain no private Drive URLs.
- **Camera — store labels:** verified logo artwork may be rendered by the existing logo components with contain positioning and its reviewed neutral backing. Keep the banner name and name-chip fallback; never substitute a sold manufacturer mark for the retailer. Exact coverage comes from the current logo registries, not a historical count. This data/docs refresh does not itself authorize layout changes.
- Breadcrumb is ประเทศไทย › จังหวัด › ย่าน › ศูนย์การค้า with the production province codes in `js/model.js#PROVINCES` (all 77, from production `base/info/lv0`); the ย่าน crumb comes from point-in-polygon on the venue's representative point; a store is the panel heading, never a crumb.
- Three row scopes are a contract: `rows` drive pins, counters and the table; `scopeRows` (filters before any area selection) drive the Aha denominator, the choropleth, the charts and the exclusive/shared reference.
- One marker always represents one outlet; never aggregate stores.
- **Camera — camera-sales scope:** approved banners and represented banners are different counts. Qualify a new category-killer outlet only with branch-specific camera-selling evidence. Record undated manufacturer sales listings/current retailer intersections as source-listed, with current stock/field operation unknown. Keep all generic, announcement-only, stock-zero and excluded network observations in private `16_NETWORK_CANDIDATES`; never add them to public rows or totals. Preserve existing IDs and reviewer fields.
- Public files carry no phone numbers (the data build scrubs them from research notes) and no Drive/private URLs.
- Keep generic caveats and research workflow residue out of ordinary UI; preserve provenance and methodology in source documentation. Preserve factual status (including the hold labels: ตัวตนหน้าร้านรอตรวจ, รวมรายการรอตรวจ, ที่ตั้งขัดกัน, ที่อยู่ติดต่อ), dates, sources and coordinate precision; never show missing as zero.
- Use approved LDS Rounded outline navigation glyphs (FILL 0, weight 300, GRAD 0). The menu icon is for the mobile data sheet only.
- Copy: short, natural Thai, no caveats on screen; units, provenance and limits live in the data section, help and docs. Neighborhood labels are ink chips (`--area-label-*`); pin name chips are light cards so they never look like area labels.
- Charts: matrix cells are equal in size; only the number and its count class carry meaning. No CSV download control. Review actual Thai at desktop and narrow widths in light and dark themes before claiming a change is done; local renders are not proof of a live release — run `npm run verify:live`.
